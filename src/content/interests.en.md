## What I'm like

Emotional, sociable, quick to strike up a conversation with strangers. I like teamwork not because job ads say so, but because working alone is slower and duller.

I am also fairly conservative: change takes me a while to get used to. Sometimes it holds me back; sometimes it saves me from impulsive decisions.

Leadership is there, but it does not switch on by itself. At school I was the class representative and did a lot beyond my own class — and it went well. In my first year at university, with studies taking all my energy, I deliberately put that aside: I had to sort out my own problems first. I want to come back to it once they stop taking up all my time.
