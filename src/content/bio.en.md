## Where I'm from

I grew up in Kirov. What school gave me was simple: I like maths — not for the elegance of proofs, but because a problem has a verifiable answer. I came to software for the same reason: maths turns into working code, and code turns into a salary. I won't pretend money doesn't matter to me; it does, and that is more honest than a speech about my passion for technology.

## Why St Petersburg

In August 2025 I started Software Engineering at the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics of St Petersburg University, and moved into a dorm next to the faculty the same month. Three criteria: the university's reputation, how much the field is in demand, and what people already studying there told me. St Petersburg was a separate argument — I like the city, and a lot of people close to me live here, so the move was never a leap into the unknown.

## What turned out to be true

The first year was not about code: it was maths, discrete mathematics, and staying afloat among deadlines. When my studies were going badly, I had to put my ambitions aside and deal with my own problems first — which turned out to be a skill of its own. The second year is more interesting: there is a team, there are projects, and there is work where you can see the result instead of a grade in a record book.
