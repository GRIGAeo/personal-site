## Where I'm from

I grew up in Kirov. What school gave me was simple: I like maths — not for the elegance of proofs, but because a problem has a verifiable answer. I came to software for the same reason: maths turns into working code, and code turns into a salary. I won't pretend money doesn't interest me; it does, and that is the reason I do what I do.

## Why St Petersburg

In August 2025 I started Software Engineering at the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics of St Petersburg University and moved into a dorm in Peterhof the same month. Three criteria: the university's reputation, how much the field is in demand, and what people already studying there told me. St Petersburg was a separate argument — I like the city, and a lot of people close to me live here.
